The process

Most apps just let you trade. Nexus makes it a process you can check.

01
Plan

The Thesis Engine sizes the trade — entry, stop, targets by R-multiple, funding cost, live P&L. The AI can draft it. You confirm. Publish and it’s timestamped on-chain — a call you can’t backdate.

02
Run

An agent trades your rules 24/7 — a preset or one you built. Starts in paper, goes live only if you say so. Hard caps, daily-loss limit, kill switch. Order-only keys that can’t withdraw.

03
Prove

Calls and live agents are graded from public price — first touch of target vs. stop, real settled trades. The ledger root is anchored on Arbitrum. Don’t trust a screenshot; inspect the contract.

Safety

Non-custodial by default. The agent can’t run off with your money.

Order-only keys

The agent’s key is derived to place orders only — it physically cannot withdraw. Blast radius is trading, nothing else.

Hard caps & kill switch

Daily-loss cap, max trades per day, leverage ceiling — all enforced. One tap kills the agent and closes the position.

You confirm before live

Paper is the default and needs no keys. Going live takes an explicit confirmation from you — every time.

What Nexus is not

Not a signal group. Not a custodial bot. Not a backtest PDF.

You can’t fake the score — it’s graded from public price, and anyone can inspect it on-chain.
