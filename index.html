FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Nexus Trading Labs?

An on-chain trading terminal built on Orderly Network ↗. Read the market with live intelligence, size a trade with real risk math in The Nexus Thesis Engine, then run it yourself or hand it to an autonomous agent — and every call is graded from public price, not a screenshot.

Do you ever hold my funds or keys?

Never. Nexus is non-custodial end to end — you trade from your own wallet and settlement happens on Orderly’s on-chain infrastructure. If you automate, the agent gets an order-only key that can place trades but can never withdraw.

Do I need an account or KYC?

No. Connect a wallet and you’re in — no sign-up, no KYC. You can explore every public board (the feed, leaderboards, wallet x-ray) before connecting anything.

What does “graded from public price” mean?

Every thesis and every agent trade is scored objectively from public market price — first touch of target vs. stop — not a self-reported screenshot. The track-record ledger is hashed and anchored on Arbitrum, so anyone can recompute and verify it. A record you can’t fake.

Can I try it without risking money?

Yes — start in paper mode. The full workflow and the agent run on simulated fills with zero funds at risk. Go live only when you decide to.

Is the autonomous agent safe to run?

It trades your rules inside hard limits — a daily-loss cap, a max trades-per-day, and a one-tap kill switch. Its key is order-only (trade, never withdraw), and you can pause or stop it anytime. Start in paper; go live on your terms.

What is $NEXUS?

A community token on Base. It unlocks cosmetic and access perks only — no yield, no revenue share, no dividends. Nexus PRO is a straightforward software subscription; $NEXUS is not an investment.
