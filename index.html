The process

A trade, start to finish — and a grade you don’t write yourself.

01
Plan

the thesis engine. you pick the levels — entry, stop, targets — and it sizes the trade: R:R, funding cost, live P&L. publish it and it’s timestamped on-chain.

02
Run

trade it yourself, or hand it to the desk — a bot on your rules. paper first if you want. order-only keys, and they stay yours.

03
Prove

the grade comes off public price — real fills, first touch of target vs. stop. the ledger root’s anchored on arbitrum. inspect it.

Safety

Your keys, your call.

Non-custodial

connect and trade direct. your funds never leave your wallet — nothing to deposit with us.

Order-only, if you automate

hand a trade to the desk and its key can only place orders — never withdraw. paper needs no key at all.

Kill it anytime

hard caps, a daily-loss limit, and a one-tap kill that flattens the position. going live is your call, every time.

the tape grades you. that’s the whole thing.
