Paper mode

The full workflow and the agent, on simulated fills. No funds at risk.

What it is

  • Paper trades fill at the live market price. Your stop, target and time limits run the same way they do live.
  • Every new agent starts in paper. Going live is your call, and a separate confirmation.
  • Paper and live records never mix. A paper result never shows up as a live one.

What paper leaves out

  • Paper P&L has no fees and no funding. It reads better than live would.
  • The app shows an after-costs line next to it: a taker fee on both legs and the funding each position paid.

How a signal earns live money

  • A signal that clears its bar gets its own paper agent.
  • It runs 30 paper trades. Each one is listed with its R. No dollar total is shown until the sample is complete.
  • A parity check confirms the agent traded what the grade grades, hour by hour.
  • Only then is a live plan written: size, daily cap, kill rule. A person signs it.
